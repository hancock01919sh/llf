# Arena v2, round 3: prompt p3, variance, and fixes (Oct 14, 2026, BD3244-37)

**Author:** Art Longbottom (with Copilot). **Status:** eval evidence for OPEN-1. This is not a recommendation: the provider pick is Ad Council's decision.

This follows [`recipes-2026-10.md`](recipes-2026-10.md). It tightens the labels, the matcher, and the prompt, then re-runs both arenas. It also answers that report's two open items: whether the new prompt hurts photos, and how much scores vary from run to run.

## 1. Summary

- **Use prompt `p3`, not `p2`.** `p2` fixed typed dish names but **hurt photos** for some models: GPT-6.1 Sol fell from 87.2 / 88.1 to 75.3 / 78.6, and Haiku from 83.3 / 83.8 to 73.3 / 76.9. `p3` keeps `p2`'s text gains and matches `p1` on photos within run-to-run noise for every shortlisted cell (§3.1; `p1` re-run on the same labels). **9 of the 15 cells meet G2 on photos** (8 with `p1`; Sonnet is the gain). Haiku is the one model still worse with `p3` than with `p1`, and it fails G2 either way. Every cell except 3.8 Flash with default thinking scores **≥ 99% / ≥ 98%** on recipes, links, and typed text.
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash with thinking off (`effort: none`) is the best value within the cap.** Across 5 photo runs it averages **95.4% precision / 95.4% recall** (about 96% / 96% without its one timeout). It rejected **100% of non-food images in all 5 runs**, at **$73/month** at 50K. It scores **100 / 100** on recipes. Thinking off is cheaper than the default ($93), has a lower p95 (3.6–5.8 s vs 5.7–9.6 s), and times out far less.
- **What Opus adds over it:** about 3 points of recall on photos (98.5% vs 95.4%), at **5.6× the cost** ($408/mo). Precision is the same (94.4% vs 95.4%).
- **Scores are stable.** Over 5 runs, the top cells vary by at most 3 points on precision and 4 on recall. The variance comes from the occasional timeout, not from the model changing its answers. The ranking doesn't change.
- **Cheap tiers:** Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite ($23/mo) averages 96.1 / 91.5 but rejects only **85% of non-food images** (81–88%). Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite ($29/mo) averages 95.3 / 83.2, with recall close to the 80% bar. Both fail the proposed non-food bar (≥ 90%) in at least one run.
- **Fixed this round:** a matcher bug that read "red chili pepper" as a bell pepper; "beef roast" not matching beef; regional names; Gemini thinking cells; and recording the provider's HTTP status on errors. All of these fixes are shared with production (§2).

## 2. What changed

### 2.1 Prompt `p3` (eval candidate)

`p3` (`services/find-my-tips/src/extract/prompt.ts`) is `p2` plus three rules:

1. **US English names**, with regional examples ("mince" → ground beef, "courgette" → zucchini, "sweet pepper" → bell pepper).
2. **For a recipe, list only the ingredients the recipe itself uses.** Skip optional variations, serving suggestions, and other recipes on the page. On the BBC Shakshuka page, the extras (chorizo, olives, falafel, bread) came from the recipe's own "you could add" and "serve with" lines, not from page chrome. Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite went from 8 extras to 0 there.
3. **For a dish, include the sauces and cheese that are part of it**, e.g. the tomato sauce on a pizza.

`p1` is still the default (`PROMPT_VERSION`). The prompt version is part of the URL cache key, so prompts are never edited in place.

### 2.2 Matcher fixes (production code, TDD §7.5)

- **Prefer adjacent words.** In the token-set step, a catalog name whose words sit together in the term now beats one spread across it, and then the longest wins. Before, "red chili pepper" matched the `bell-pepper` synonym "red pepper"; now it matches chili pepper.
- **"roast" is a cooking word, not the head noun**, like "roasted". "beef roast" → beef, and "roast chicken" → chicken. Two Gemini cells had lost beef on the Sunday-roast case because of this.

### 2.3 Labels and eval catalog (eval-only data)

| Fix | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Chili synonyms (chile, chilli pepper, serrano, habanero, Thai chili, …) | `eval/catalogs/eval-v2.json` | Real recipe terms that didn't match |
| Zucchini synonyms (summer squash, yellow squash, yellow zucchini) | `eval-v2.json` | "yellow squash" matched pumpkin |
| Olives removed from 3 dishes | `eval/sets/v2-mixed.json` | Checked against the photos: no olives visible |
| Watermelon acceptable on n5k-1565884692; carrot acceptable on n5k-1560367085 | `v2-mixed.json` | Visible in the photo (another plate; orange stir-fry strips) |
| Milk and butter acceptable on `meal-rotisserie` | `eval/sets/v2-recipes.json` | Usual in mashed potatoes |
| Pad thai: shrimp and herbs moved to acceptable | `v2-recipes.json` | Tofu and chicken versions are just as standard |
| Wikipedia tuna salad: celery moved to acceptable | `v2-recipes.json` | The page's main ingredients are tuna and mayonnaise; celery is only mentioned in passing |

Several labels were checked against the photo and kept, so those cases still count as model errors:
- **Egg-white scrambles** (3 dishes): models say "cheese". The crumbled whites do look like feta, but the label is right.
- **Honeydew read as pineapple** (n5k-1560367085).
- **Sausage patties read as meatballs** (n5k-1561392386).

### 2.4 Matrices and harness

- **Gemini thinking cells:** `gemini-3.8-flash` with `effort: none` (thinking budget 0) and `low` (1024 tokens) added to [`eval/arena-v2.json`](../../../eval/arena-v2.json) and [`eval/arena-recipes.json`](../../../eval/arena-recipes.json).
- **Variance matrix:** [`eval/arena-v2-top.json`](../../../eval/arena-v2-top.json), 7 cells: the 3.8 Flash variants, both Flash-Lites, gpt-5-mini, and Opus.
- **`providerStatus`** (eval only): when a provider call fails, the run JSON records its HTTP status, e.g. 503 or 429. `ToolError` now carries the SDK error as its `cause`. Users still see only the error code.

**`p1` vs `p3` on photos:** within run-to-run noise (§3.2) for most cells. `p3` is clearly better for 2.5 Flash-Lite, gpt-5.4-nano, and Sonnet precision (+4), and it rejects more non-food photos for the weak models. **Haiku is the exception** (⚠️): it loses about 6 points of precision and 5 of recall with `p2` and `p3`. It fails G2 with every prompt, so this doesn't affect the shortlist. The 3.8 Flash cells move with their error counts, not with the prompt.

**A timeout counts every expected ingredient as missed.** In the final run, 3.8/none timed out once, on a pizza with 6 expected ingredients, which cost about 3 points of recall. Without that case it scores about 96 / 96, in line with its other runs.

Readings:

- **Thinking doesn't help photos.** 3.8 Flash scores the same with thinking off, low, or default. Turning it off saves about 20% and roughly halves p95, and it removes most timeouts at our 15 s `MODEL_TIMEOUT_MS`.
- **3.8/low's 10 errors are almost all fast upstream errors** (about 200 ms). These are Gemini 429/503 responses under our parallel eval load: a direct probe returned 12 of 12, and later runs recorded 503s. The production client retries once on 502/504 (TDD §7.6). The provider decision should count these errors (OPEN-1), and the AWS-side quota needs checking with Google (OPEN-2).
- **gpt-5-mini/minimal is on the precision line** (88–91%). It meets G2 in some runs and not others.

### 3.3 What 3.8 Flash (none) still misses

The same cases are hard for every top cell, Opus included:

- **Missed:** sausage on round patties (models say meatballs); egg on an overexposed plate; egg whites read as cheese (3 dishes); olives on one salad.
- **Wrong extras:** cheese on the egg-white dishes; tomato for watermelon at the edge of the frame; pineapple for honeydew.

These are looks-alike errors, not the model inventing ingredients. In the product, the user can remove a wrong ingredient before seeing tips.

## 6. Limits

1. **Small sets:** one photo case moves recall by about 0.5 points, and one timeout by up to 3.
2. **Labels:** Nutrition5k is cafeteria food. The text labels are synthetic and drafted by Opus, so Opus's text scores are an upper bound.
3. **Variance** was measured for 7 cells (5 runs each). The other cells ran 1–2 times.
4. **Gemini errors under load** are from our parallel eval runs (up to 4 at once). Production traffic will look different; quotas need confirming with Google (OPEN-2).
5. **Stand-in catalog** (`eval-v2`, 104 slugs). Fruits such as kiwi, cherries, and dates and dishes such as meatballs aren't in it, so they show up as unmatched terms, not errors (OPEN-8).
6. **List prices** from Oct 13, 2026; no batch or caching discounts.

## 7. Recommended next steps

1. **Make `p3` the next prompt version** (`PROMPT_VERSION=p3`), replacing the plan to ship `p2`. Since the version is part of the cache key, the switch doesn't need a cache purge.
2. **If Gemini 3.8 Flash is shortlisted, run it with thinking off** (`AI_REASONING_EFFORT=none`, which sends `thinkingBudget: 0`). It costs less and responds faster, and the quality is the same.
3. **Add non-food rejection (≥ 90%) to G2** and agree the bar with Ad Council (OPEN-8). The 3.1 Flash-Lite result shows why: it passes on precision and recall but accepts about 15% of non-food photos.
4. **Count provider errors in the pick** (OPEN-1). With `providerStatus` in the run JSON, a soak test at production concurrency would show how often 429/503s happen.
5. **Carry the synonym work into the CMS catalog** (chili, zucchini and squash, tomato sauce, regional names) when it lands (OPEN-8).
