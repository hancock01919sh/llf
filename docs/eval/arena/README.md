# Model arena (BD3244-37)

**Status:** the harness is built, the ≤1 MP image resize is built, and a **PRELIMINARY live run** (Oct 12, 15 synthetic text cases, $0.04) is done: [`eval/reports/arena-2026-10-12T205919Z.md`](../../../eval/reports/arena-2026-10-12T205919Z.md). On this set Claude Haiku 4.5 and GPT-5 mini (minimal, low) meet the G2 bar; GPT-5 nano and Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite do not (injection, precision). The set is too small and synthetic to pick from; the 80–120 item labeled set is still pending (OPEN-8). No recommendation is made here (OPEN-1 is Ad Council's decision).

**Latest (Oct 13, arena-v1):** 12 current models plus Claude Opus 5.5 as the reference on `v1-mixed` (126 cases, $2.86): [`eval/reports/arena-2026-10-13T232648Z-v1.md`](../../../eval/reports/arena-2026-10-13T232648Z-v1.md). Best within the cap: Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite (88.6% / 90.2%, $28/month at 50K). No cell meets G2 yet. See PROOF_DOC P9 and the full write-up, [`model-audit-2026-10.md`](model-audit-2026-10.md).

**Latest (Oct 14, arena v2 + recipes):** with the v1 scoring artifacts fixed (catalog `eval-v2`), **7 of 13 cells meet G2 on images**. Best within the cap: Gemini 3.8 Flash (95.1% / 95.1%, $88/month at 50K); the cheapest qualifying cell is Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite (96.4% / 87.6%, $22/month). On recipe links, dish names, and pasted text, every cell scores ≥ 99% / ≥ 99% with the new prompt `p2`. Write-up: [`recipes-2026-10.md`](recipes-2026-10.md).

**Latest (Oct 14, round 3: prompt `p3` and variance):** `p2` hurt photos for some models; the new **`p3`** matches `p1` on photos (re-run on the same labels; only Haiku is worse) and **9 of 15 cells meet G2 on photos**. Over 5 runs, **Gemini 3.8 Flash with thinking off** averages **95.4% / 95.4%**, rejects 100% of non-food images, and scores 100 / 100 on recipes, at **$73/month** at 50K. That's within about 3 recall points of Opus ($408). Scores vary ≤ 3–4 points between runs. Write-up: [`p3-2026-10.md`](p3-2026-10.md).

**Latest (Oct 15, round 4: product photos, image injection, load):** on the new `v3-product` set (92 cases: synthetic receipts and lists, Commons fridge photos, 16 image-injection probes), receipts and lists score 99.6–100% precision for every model; most misses are catalog synonyms. Fridges are the hardest input: Gemini 3.8 Flash with thinking off scores 90.1% / 92.9% (Opus 94.8% / 94.0%, on Opus's own labels). **3.8 Flash and Opus resisted every image injection; 3.1 and 3.5 Flash-Lite and GPT-5 mini didn't**, so they no longer meet G2. A 1,100-call soak saw no 429s or 5xx. Budget about $206/month in 2027 if every use is a product photo. Write-up: [`product-2026-10.md`](product-2026-10.md).

**Comprehensive report (updated Oct 15):** [`model-audit-2026-10.md`](model-audit-2026-10.md) brings all three rounds together: the model audit, photo and text results, variance, what the budget buys, and next steps. Gemini 3.8 Flash's promotional price ends Dec 31, before go-live, so budget it at about **$147/month** (2027 price, thinking off, 50K uses).

This folder holds the working drafts for the Oct 8 arena action items. They feed the production provider recommendation (OPEN-1), which is due **before Nov 13**.

- **How we pick:** TDD [D24](../../TDD.md#14-decision-log). The pick is the cheapest cell that meets G2 (precision ≥ 0.9, recall ≥ 0.8; bar still to agree, OPEN-8), resists 100% of injection cases, and forecasts under the ~$500/month cap at 25K–50K uses.
- **Provider adapters:** TDD [§7.7](../../TDD.md#77-provider-abstraction). **Eval harness:** TDD §7.9 in [`TDD.md`](../../TDD.md). **Spike log:** [`PROOF_DOC.md`](../../PROOF_DOC.md) P9.
- **How to run it:** the arena section of the [Find My Tips README](../../../services/find-my-tips/README.md#arena-bd3244-37).
- **Matrix:** [`eval/arena-v1.json`](../../../eval/arena-v1.json) (`arena-v1`, Oct 2026 model audit: cheap and mid tiers, two `arena-v0` baselines, Claude Opus 5.5 as the reference). The original [`eval/arena.example.json`](../../../eval/arena.example.json) (`arena-v0`) is still the default. **Labeled sets and provenance:** [`eval/sets/README.md`](../../../eval/sets/README.md).
- **Reference cell:** a cell with `"reference": true` is a quality yardstick, not a candidate, and runs whatever it costs. The report adds **What the budget buys**, which puts every candidate next to the reference with the quality gap and monthly cost. It also adds **What it misses out on** for the best in-budget pick: ingredients it misses, wrong ingredients it adds, and non-food images it accepts, all compared with the reference, plus the largest gaps by case. This shows the client what the $500/month cap buys and what it gives up.
